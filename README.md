### About Me

I focus on building performant, low-latency, and scalable software. My work centers on systems programming, computer vision, AI, and graphics programming, alongside developing full-stack solutions.

- **Systems & Core Architecture:** Engineering low-latency and resource-efficient architectures with a focus on modern C++, hardware-level constraints, and execution pipelines.
- **Vision, AI & Graphics:** Developing real-time visual pipelines, interactive rendering, and optimizing edge AI models for efficient on-device execution.
- **Application Engineering:** Building standalone, cross-platform, and full-stack software—seamlessly bridging low-level native modules and local runtimes with modern interfaces.
